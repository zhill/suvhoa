# **SUVHOA Facilities**

<img src="./img/Pool Zoom In.jpg" alt="clubhouse" width="1400">

### Homeowners and tenants are welcome to use the clubhouse, pool, tennis court, basketball court and playground. Your key card will open 
the gates for both the swimming pool area and tennis courts. 

# Swimming Pool 
### The pool hours are from 6:00 am to 10:00 pm.

# Tennis Courts
### Tennis courts are available year-round from sunrise to sundown.

# Playground, Basketball Court and Volleyball Court
### These three areas are not gated and available to the neighborhood.
