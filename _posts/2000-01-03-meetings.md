# **Board Meetings**
### SUVHOA Board Meetings are scheduled for every second Wednesday of each month at 6:30 p.m.

## Meeting Location 

### Meetings are currently held at the SUVHOA Clubhouse at 7290 Davenport Rd., Goleta, CA 93117

## Meeting Information 

### Board members meet on a monthly basis to discuss neighborhood issues, amenities maintenance and repairs, and more. 

### Homeowners are welcome to attend our meetings.

**Please visit the [TeamHOA Portal](https://teamhoa.com) for agendas, minutes, and financials.**
